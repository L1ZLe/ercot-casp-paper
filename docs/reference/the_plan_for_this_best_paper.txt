## 1. First Principles — WHY this problem
### 1.1 The market rule (the physics we exploit)
An ERCOT day-ahead locational marginal price at a node `LMP = λ(energy) + congrestion + losses`. A **spread** between two settlement points `A`, `B` cancels the energy term:
```
spread_AB = (congestion_A − congestion_B) + (loss_A − loss_B)
```
Congestion is driven by **binding transmission constraints**, each with a **shadow price μ_k** ($/MWh, cost of relieving 1 MW). The standard congestion formula rewrites the spread as:
```
congestion spread = Σ_k ΔSF_k · μ_k
```
where `ΔSF_k` = difference in shift factors (fraction of 1 MW at a node that flows on constraint k), `μ_k` = shadow price. **This is the market settlement rule — not a black-box input.**

### 1.2 The scientific gap (why this paper)
- **Anchor (Yu et al. 2026)**: market-rule-informed NN for *European imbalance prices* — 1 imbalance price per zone, ex-post. Never tested on the US nodal single-settlement market.
- **Gap 1 — market-structure transfer.** Does rule-embedding generalize to a fundamentally different market design (nodal, ex-ante)? Open.
- **Gap 2 — real constraint data.** ERCOT publishes actual DAM shadow prices, binding constraint IDs, violation amounts. No neural forecast uses these real regulatory filings as structured inputs. (We do.)
- **Gap 3 — interpretability.** Rule-embedding should give interpretability by construction: attention over constraints = "which line is squeezing the spread?"

### 1.3 Why the *spread* target
- Energy (λ) cancels → cleaner, simpler, purely a congestion task.
- **Directly tradable** (PTP spread) → matches your MRP production system and proves quant value.
- Shows first-principles domain understanding (not just a big-net fit).

## 3. METHOD — the model (first principles → architecture)
### 3.1 Input features per hour
- **Constraint slots**: top `K=50` binding constraints sorted by `shadowPrice` desc, padded. Per-slot: `[shadowPrice, constraintId_idx (embedding index, PAD=0), kV_level=max(fromKV,toKV)/345, flow_ratio=constraintValue/limit clipped[0,5]]` → `[B,50,4]`.
- **Temporal**: Fourier bearings of hour/day-of-week (6) + lagged spreads t−24/48/168 → 18-d.
- **Path embedding**: 8-d learned source/sink identity (transfer to other pairs).
### 3.2 Constraint-attention (the learned-ΔSF)
```
query  = Linear(path_embedding(src|sink))
attn   = softmax( bmm(query, slot_keys) / sqrt(d) )
mu_k   = Linear(slot_encoder(slots))          # real shadow-price slots
ΔSF_k  = attn
spread = Σ_k ΔSF_k · mu_k                     # energy term canceled
```
### 3.3 Head & loss
- `quantile_head(Linear→7)` outputs q ∈ {0.10,0.25,0.45,0.50,0.55,0.75,0.90}.
- Loss = **Average Quantile Loss (AQL)** + λ=0.1 · **LA-CASF** non-crossing penalty.

### 3.4 Honest note (ADR-worthy)
- The head is a **plain Linear**; non-crossing is **soft-encouraged** by LA-CASF, **not guaranteed by construction**.
- → We must **measure** crossing rate empirically, not assert it. (E6 below.)
